## Findings (the mechanism)

- **Two-key lock** — slowdown requires BOTH a brands filter AND a multi-path GPC prefix. Prod medians: brand+multi-path 122ms / brand+leaf 23ms / GPC-only 30ms / brand-only 24ms / none 22ms. Leaf categories are baseline **regardless of size** (Rugs 4.2M docs = 28ms). Cost is result-independent (0-ad = 100-ad) → filter evaluation, not search or ranking.
- **Brand is the trigger via its tiny null-sentinel population**: unset brand = 0.96% of corpus vs gender 26.5% / age 28.9% / condition 8.0% / GPC 9.7%. Clause estimate ≈ f(brand)+0.0096 lands at/below approximate-threshold 0.01 → planner leaves the ANN fast path; the multi-term GPC prefix OR (no rank:filter) is then evaluated directly at 50–140ms. Large-sentinel filters can't flip the regime — GPC+cga measured benign (41ms avg), as predicted.
- **Stage↔prod reversal explained**: same flip on stage, but direct evaluation over a tiny corpus is fast (6.5ms) — stage masks the regime entirely.
- **Latency histogram is trimodal** (22 / ~55 / ~127ms bands) = discrete evaluation regimes; band membership is a near-deterministic function of the GPC value once brand is present.
- Open detail: within multi-path, cost groups by category branch (Clothing subcats ~139 even at 36 docs; Shoes 124; H&B ~112–120; Electronics ~56) — not explained by docs-under-prefix or term count. Needs live searchCoverage/trace or the Vespa vendor.
- Fix candidates (leverage order): (1) drop the `""` sentinel from brands / move unset-matching to `hasBrand`/`hasGpc` bools with rank:filter; (2) GPC exact IN (`vespaYqlVersionProduct=1` already implements it) or materialized path array; (3) rank:filter on googleProductCategory.
