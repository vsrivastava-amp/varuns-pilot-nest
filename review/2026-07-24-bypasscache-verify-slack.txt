Verified in the code: yes. bypassCache only skips the cache *read* — every query then goes through the LLM and hits the normal save path, which is an unconditional DynamoDB put_item on the same key (sha256(evalId, normalized query)). So the recalculated values overwrite the old entries in place. One nuance: results that fail or come back with invalid GPC/IAB codes are not saved, so those keys keep their old cached value rather than getting clobbered — the refresh only overwrites successful extractions. Checked on main, i.e. what prod is running.
