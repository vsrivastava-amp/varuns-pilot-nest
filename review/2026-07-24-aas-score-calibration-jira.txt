Title: Calibrate unified retrieval score coefficients (kvssScoreLinearA/B) before 3.0 text-ads go-live

Suggested: project AI, child of AI-1513 (AAS supports 3.0 Text Ads); link AI-1435, AI-1550.

Description:

Discover 3.0 ranks product and text ads together on normalizedScore (DiscoverRankingStep). A text ad's normalizedScore is floor((a * rawKvssScore + b) * 1e6) with defaults a=0.93, b=0 (KvssProperties.ScoreNormalization; per-request override via experiment keys kvssScoreLinearA/kvssScoreLinearB). Product ads use floor(rawVespaScore * 1e6). These coefficients therefore directly set the text-vs-product mix of every 3.0 auction — and per the current plan DSP multiplies the unified score into the auction (unifiedScore x CTR x CPC), so they propagate into pricing/yield, not just ordering.

The current defaults have no recorded derivation. History (ad-auction-service PR #44 / AI-1435):
- 2026-07-10: introduced as a=1 (identity)
- 2026-07-14: a=0.8 in KvssProperties and a=0.93 in application.yml, simultaneously
- 2026-07-15: aligned to 0.93 after a CR comment flagged the mismatch
- KvssProperties javadoc still states the rationale in terms of a=0.8 ("discount KVSS's raw score flat" because VSS full-title cosine similarity is a stronger relevance signal than KVSS short-phrase match)

The directional rationale (text discounted below product at equal raw score) may well be right — but 0.93 vs 0.8 vs anything else is currently uncalibrated, and going live silently bakes it in.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Calibration method agreed and run — e.g. aligning the raw score distributions of KVSS vs VSS on matched traffic, and/or fitting against a relevance ground truth (ARES-by-ad-type currently scores product ads well above text ads and could anchor this).
2. Defaults (a and b) set from that analysis; decide whether b=0 survives.
3. KvssProperties javadoc, application.yml, and the recorded rationale all agree.
4. Decision recorded before 3.0 text ads ramp beyond experiment traffic.
