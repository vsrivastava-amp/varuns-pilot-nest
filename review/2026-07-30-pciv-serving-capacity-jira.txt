Online pCIV needs enough model-serving throughput to survive launch traffic, and needs to degrade gracefully when it runs out. Two halves.

*Quota increase*
* At a 200 QPS target we need roughly 12k RPM. Default is 10k, so even the friendliest OSS quota needs a raise before we are launch-capable.
* Blocker to resolve first: which AWS account do stage and prod EKS pods run in? Quotas are per-account, so the increase has to land in the serving account. Dev access was verified in 564079877134 — need to confirm whether that is the same one.
* The ask should carry measured tokens per request and a 2–3x headroom framing.

*Client-side throttling*
* Request and token limiters smoothed to RPM/60 and TPM/60, a bounded queue, at most one deadline-aware retry, and shedding rather than amplifying under pressure.
* This is not hypothetical: AI-1598 reports llm-evaluator-service failing persistently on rate limiting, and online pCIV is a second deployment of the same image.
