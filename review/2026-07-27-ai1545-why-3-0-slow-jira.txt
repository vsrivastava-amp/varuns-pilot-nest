Analysis of the prod run (c170901), from the attached CSVs, the pla_gold corpus tables, and the deployed schema. The prod numbers are real. The cost has one specific shape.

Caveat first: prod VSS→Vespa timeout is 150 ms. The observed max is 148 ms. 46/1000 of the 3.0 rows recorded no latency (timeouts and failures, excluded from all averages). The reported p95/p99 are floors, not estimates.

The slowdown needs two conditions at the same time:
1. A brands filter is present. Any brand — Nike, adidas, or a brand that matches zero docs.
2. The googleProductCategories value prefix-matches more than one category path in the corpus.

Paired comparison over the 1000 prod rows (same query term, both request versions):

| 3.0 filter shape | n | 2.0 avg ms | 3.0 avg ms |
| no filters | 264 | 22.3 | 21.9 |
| brand and/or gender/age/condition, no GPC | 149 | 22.5 | 24.6 |
| single-path GPC + brand | 77 | 24.8 | 23.4 |
| single-path GPC, no brand | 90 | 23.0 | 30.6 |
| multi-path GPC, no brand | 159 | 23.7 | 36.5 |
| multi-path GPC + brand | 209 | 23.0 | 110.2 |

Doc volume under the category does not matter. A 4.2M-doc single-path category costs the same as a 200-doc one. Result count does not matter: 0-ad rows cost the same as 100-ad rows. The cost is filter evaluation, not search or ranking.

Why brand is the trigger (from corpus stats and schema; needs live confirmation): each 3.0 filter matches "value X OR field unset". Unset brand covers 0.96% of docs. All other unset populations are larger (condition 8%, category 9.7%, gender 26%, age 29%). The rank profile approximate-threshold is 0.01. A rare brand plus the 0.96% sentinel drops the estimated filter selectivity below that threshold. Vespa then leaves the ANN fast path and evaluates the filter directly. In that mode, the multi-path category prefix (a field with no rank:filter) costs 50–140 ms.

The same mechanism exists on stage. Stage's corpus is small, so direct evaluation there is fast (6.5 ms avg). That is why the slow slice reversed between stage and prod.

footgun: Do not tune filters from stage latency numbers. The slow slice reverses between stage and prod. Also: these samples contain zero price and currency filters. Those two shapes exceeded the 150 ms timeout in the 7/24 combination probe. Real pCIV traffic will send price filters. price and salePrice have no fast-search; currencyCode has no fast-search either.
