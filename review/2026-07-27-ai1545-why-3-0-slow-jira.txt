Hey @Artem Dippel — dug through the prod CSVs the same way as the stage ones (cc @Saksham Bhatla). The pattern is even cleaner here: 3.0 slows down only when a brands filter is present AND the GPC value prefix-matches more than one category path. Everything else runs at parity with 2.0 or faster — no-filter rows 21.9ms vs 22.3, brand + single-path category 23.4 vs 24.8. Multi-path GPC + brand: 110ms avg vs 23. Result count doesn't matter (0-ad rows cost the same as 100-ad rows), and the tail is clipped by the 150ms timeout — 46/1000 rows recorded no latency at all, so the published p95/p99 are floors.

Best explanation so far (needs live confirmation): the "or not populated" branch again, through a different door. Unset brand is only ~1% of the corpus — right at the rank profile's approximate-threshold (0.01) — so a brand clause for any rare brand estimates as ultra-selective, and Vespa leaves the ANN path to evaluate the filter directly. That's where the multi-path category prefix gets expensive (googleProductCategory has no rank:filter). Unset gender/age/condition are 8–29% of docs, which is why those filters never trigger it. Stage flips the same way, but direct evaluation over a small corpus is fast — that's why the slow slice reversed between stage (GPC-no-brand) and prod (GPC+brand).

Couple of things worth deciding:

- Same question as before, sharper now: is matching unset-brand products worth this cost? Dropping that one sentinel — or moving it to a hasBrand bool with rank:filter — defuses the whole thing. Exact IN for categories (vespaYqlVersionProduct=1) would also do it.
- I'd like to confirm the mechanism with searchCoverage diagnostics on the PROD-TEST cluster before we treat it as settled — happy to run that.

One flag for the latency-budget discussion: none of the 1000 rows carried price or currency filters — the two shapes that blew the 150ms timeout in your combination probe — and real pCIV traffic will send price ranges. (price/salePrice have no fast-search; neither does currencyCode, which probably explains why currency showed up heavy in that probe.)
