Hey @Bhupesh, I had some context on this one, so I pulled the Datadog history and read the SSP client code. Dumping it all here so the ticket does not start from zero.

What the monitor measures

It triggers above 5 matching log events in a rolling 5-minute window. It recovers at 5 or fewer. It counts log lines, not requests.

The filter is an OR, not an AND. The query ends with @stack_trace:(*HttpTimeoutException* *IntentIdentifierControllerApi*), and the space inside the parentheses means OR. Last week it matched 4,393 logs. Only 3,681 of those contain HttpTimeoutException. So roughly 712 of them are other intent-identification failures. Replacing the space with AND turns it into a timeout monitor. Leaving it as OR makes it a general "intent identification failed" monitor, which may be what you want, but the name says otherwise.

Volume last week (2026-07-20 18:00 to 2026-07-27 18:00 UTC)

18 episodes, 4,393 matching logs. Median episode 5 minutes, longest 20.

The volume is not spread out. 683 of 07-27's 692 logs landed in the single minute 14:50. 07-26 has the same shape at 08:46. Every other hour in the week is single digits or low tens. So the week is two one-minute bursts plus a constant 1 to 2 failures per minute.

The constant background failures come from normal latency

The service's routine max latency runs 22 to 57 ms. application.yml sets read-timeout-millis to 30 by default. Prod can override that with an env var and I did not read the deployed env, but the observed abandon time of 30.20 ms matches the default. So a slice of ordinary traffic crosses the deadline every hour. A sampled failure finished server-side at 52 to 54 ms, after SSP had already closed the stream.

The bursts are stalls, not gradual latency growth

p50 stayed at 5 ms through both bursts. One request during the 07-26 burst took 6.09 seconds. p95 did not move at all that minute (0.0154, flat against its normal band).

The client retries timeouts with no wait between attempts

IntentIdentifierServiceConfiguration builds the Resilience4j retry with maxAttempts = retries + 1 and waitDuration 0. retryExceptions includes IOException, and HttpTimeoutException extends IOException, so timeouts do retry. retries defaults to 1, so each failure sends 2 attempts back to back. IntentFilter logs "Unexpected error during intent identification" once per request, after both attempts fail. So the 683 logs in that minute represent roughly 1,366 calls sent to a service that was already stalled.

A rate metric already exists

sspEngine.intent.identifier.errors is live and carries a kind tag. It increments once per request. Last week in ric1: request_timeout 3,657, stream_exhausted 341, connect_timeout 96, io_error 31, service_error 14. During the 07-27 14:50 burst, kind:request_timeout recorded 683, which matches the log count exactly. A failure-rate monitor can be built on this today, dividing it by the intent identifier call count. No new instrumentation is needed.

footgun: episode duration does not indicate severity here. The 683-timeout burst produced a 5-minute episode, the same length as episodes containing six logs. 13 of the 18 episodes lasted exactly 5 minutes, which is only the monitor's evaluation window.
