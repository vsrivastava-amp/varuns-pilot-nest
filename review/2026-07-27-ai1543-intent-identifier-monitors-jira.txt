The monitor counts log lines, not requests. It triggers above 5 matching events in a rolling 5-minute window and recovers at 5 or fewer. An episode therefore tells you the count crossed a line. It does not tell you how many requests failed.

Last week (2026-07-20 18:00 to 2026-07-27 18:00 UTC): 18 episodes, 4,393 matching logs, median episode 5 minutes, longest 20. The volume is not spread out. 683 of 07-27's 692 logs landed in the single minute 14:50, and 07-26 has the same shape at 08:46. Every other hour in the week is single digits or low tens. This looks like two one-minute bursts plus a constant drip of 1 to 2 per minute — possibly two different problems behind one alert.

Two observations that may matter for scoping:

1. The drip looks structural. The service's routine max latency runs 22 to 57 ms against SSP's approximately 30 ms deadline, so a slice of normal traffic exceeds the deadline as a matter of course. A sampled failure completed server-side at 52 to 54 ms.
2. The bursts look like stalls, not latency creep. p50 is 5 ms, but one request during the 07-26 burst took 6.09 seconds. p95 did not move during that minute (0.0154, flat against baseline).
