Title: Evaluate gpt-5-6-luna for offline CIV and ARES

Suggested placement: project AI, child of AI-1473 (ML/AI: Emerging Business Issues).

Description:

OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 pricing on 2026-07-30. Luna now costs about the same as gpt-5-4-nano (https://admarketplace.slack.com/archives/C08GKCC9742/p1785468944971119). Offline CIV runs gpt-5-4-nano today and ARES runs gpt-5-mini. So Luna is a possible quality gain on one and a cost cut on the other. Neither has been measured.

Add gpt-5-6-luna to the llm-evaluator-service and run both domains against their current active configs on the same inputs. Compare quality, latency and measured cost, then recommend whether to switch either one.

The CIV side needs no infra work. INFRA-3462 created civ-gpt-5-6-luna on 2026-07-24. ARES needs an ares-gpt-5-6-luna endpoint, which nobody has requested yet.

Acceptance criteria:

1. Offline CIV and ARES quality for Luna against the current models on the same inputs.
2. Latency and measured cost per domain, with prompt caching accounted for.
3. A recommendation on whether to switch either domain's active eval.
