## Discover 3.0 contract debate

A 19-reply thread in `#proj-amp-discover-3-0` litigated how intent type drives matching, and it lands on the pCIV prompt. Artem asked whether `intent.targets[].ad_count` and `placements[].ad_count` are mutually exclusive. Norbert pushed for one fallback chain across inline and adjacent: "I recommend using the placement.name -> topic -> prompt fallback logic for both Inline and Adjacent." Saksham resisted letting publishers steer extraction: "Our pCIV prompts will also become complicated… We should instead have them send all the *context* they have, and optionally, tell us the *publisher* *intent*."

Dhaval settled it at 10:26: prioritize by ad type rather than placement type. "for product ads is product.name -> chat.topic -> chat.prompt and for text ads is chat.topic -> chat.prompt (and maybe product.name in the end?)… Which ad count is present purely dictates merging and whether to rank combined or separately." He noted product.name is too specific for keyword matching, "eg. Airmax 50 is likely not a keyword but running shoes is", and that they will A/B test field choice at scale.

This answers Norbert's fallback-validation ask that went unanswered yesterday, and it constrains what the pCIV prompt must emit.
